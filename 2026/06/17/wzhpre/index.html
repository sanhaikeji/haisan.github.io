UGreen Cloud-space: A better choice for cloud-space

Have you ever been anxious for a big and free cloud-space?

Have you erer used Baidu Cloud-space or Quark Cloud-space and been angry when your VIP was due?

Let’s use UGreen Cloud-Space!

The common aspects of UGreen Cloud-space

UGreen Cloud-space is a website which can memorize your files which you want to upload.

Like other cloud-spaces, UGreen Cloud-space enable you to upload or download your files whenever you click choice “Upload” or “Download”.

Certainly, to operate, you should have an active account.

You can find the admin of UGreen Cloud-space to create a new active account for you.

And if you upload something that break the law or the rules of UGreen Cloud-space, or you make an admin upset, your account will be inactive.

Features of UGreen Cloud-space

Firstly, UGreen Cloud-space has a big feature second to none – the size limit of the sum of your files that you upload and the speed limit of the speed when you download your files.

UGreen Cloud-space has a size about 20TB to memorize your files.

And it has not got anyone that is VIP of itself.

That means all the features of UGreen Cloud-space is free!

Comparing UGreen Cloud-space and other cloud-space, such as Luogu Team’s Files which has a size limit about 10MB and a size limit for VIP accounts about 1GB, and Baidu Cloud-space which has a difficult process for downloading files and lose your file whenever you has not got a VIP account, you can find how great and nice UGreen Cloud-space is!

UGreen Cloud-space also has a quick speed to download your files.

you can compare others and it.

For example, Baidu Cloud-space has the slowest(this is a joke.) speed to download your files.

A big file about 10GB can killing time about 1 day!

But UGreen Cloud-space doesn’t has this problem.

This file will waste time about 1~5 hours!

What a fantastic wbesite is!(All of us are crying and proud of this website.)

Another feature is that you can look through all you files that is not strange in UGreen Cloud-space online.

For example, you can look through a text file and update them online.

And when you upload a films like RMS Titanic, Ready Player One or A Perfect Match, you can has a high-clearance for watching your films.

But if you use Baidu Cloud-space, I can only wish you have a lucky trip.

The last feature is that you can build your own website on UGreen Cloud-space because it has a lot of useful tools.

A famous example is that haisan.club .

some disability of UGreen Cloud-space

There is only one disability of UGreen Cloud-space – your account should have a long,safe,difficult key , which maybe you will forget your key someday.

This disability is made by me but I am forced to do this by Iahnas.

An interesting extra Game – the War of Admins!

it have been made by us in last July.

it was really boring but can kill your time.

UGreen Cloud-space’s admins can update other users’ keys including other admins’ keys!

So Surely, there is a war of admins.

their goals is that they update other admins’ keys before others operate.

If all the admins’ keys except yours is not the keys they set, you get the King of UGreen Cloud-space.

Thank you for listening!
